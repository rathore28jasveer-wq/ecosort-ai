The Real-World Problem

Everyday consumers struggle to differentiate composite packaging, recyclable plastics, and hazardous goods. Unsegregated waste contaminates entire recycling trucks, forcing over 60% of recyclable materials into toxic open dumps and incinerators.

The AI-Powered Solution

EcoSort AI provides accessible machine intelligence via text description or photo preview. It determines waste streams, explains chemical or mechanical recycling reasons, flags safety alerts, and suggests circular upcycling possibilities.

UN SDG 12 & 11 Impact

Target 12.5 focuses on waste reduction through prevention, recycling, and reuse, while Target 11.6 minimizes adverse per-capita urban environmental impact by addressing municipal solid waste segregation.

Sustainable Habits & Education

Waste Knowledge Hub

Practical, science-backed guidance to empower households and students to practice circular resource management.

Organic & Wet Waste

Compostable

Food scraps, vegetable peels, leaves, coffee grounds, and tea leaves. When sent to anaerobic landfills, wet waste rots and generates potent methane gas.

Best Practice:

Segregate in green wet bins or use household compost pits/bokashi buckets to create nutrient-rich soil humus.

Recyclable Dry Materials

Circular

Newspapers, magazines, cardboard boxes, paper bags, metal beverage cans, and clean glass bottles. Paper can be repulped 5 to 7 times.

Best Practice:

Ensure materials are clean and dry. Flatten cardboard boxes and crush soda cans to maximize storage and transport volume.

Plastic Waste

Resin Codes 1-7

PET bottles, milk jugs (HDPE), shampoo bottles, and containers. Single-use non-recyclable multi-layered sachets break down into hazardous microplastics.

Best Practice:

Rinse residual liquids. Check bottom for resin codes (#1 PET and #2 HDPE have the highest recycling value).

E-Waste (Electronics)

Critical Stream

Mobile chargers, cables, circuit boards, obsolete phones, and computer peripherals. Contributes over 70% of heavy metals in landfills.

Best Practice:

Never discard with regular garbage. Hand over to authorized EPR buy-back programs or retail electronic kiosks.

Domestic Hazardous

High Danger

Batteries, paint cans, solvent tins, aerosol sprays, chemical bleaches, and broken glass shards. Threatens sanitation workers with lacerations and caustic burns.

Best Practice:

Wrap sharp broken glass in cardboard; tape battery terminals; store chemicals in original labeled jars for designated collection.

General & Sanitary

Inert / Residual

Soiled diapers, sanitary napkins, ceramic mugs, thermal receipts, and dust swept from floors. Unrecoverable via standard recycling channels.

Best Practice:

Wrap sanitary items separately in paper marked with a red dot. Place in black or dark gray residual municipal bins.

AI Ethics, Trust & Governance

Responsible AI Considerations

As required by the 1M1B and IBM SkillsBuild framework, EcoSort AI incorporates ethical AI governance to guarantee user safety, privacy, fairness, and accountability.

1. Fairness & Inclusivity

The model avoids socioeconomic assumptions and provides universally accessible waste guidance. Whether a user describes a premium smartphone or local scrap plastic, instructions adhere to objective material science without bias.

2. Transparency & Rationale

Every classification provides an explicit explainability reasoning card ("Why"). We demystify the AI black box by explaining polymer resin characteristics, combustion hazards, or biological decomposition requirements.

3. Privacy-by-Design

Uploaded images are held ephemerally in browser client-side memory using the HTML5 FileReader API. No user photographs, GPS coordinates, or identifiable personal telemetry are stored in external tracking databases.

4. Safety-First Warnings

Whenever hazardous goods (such as lithium battery fire risks, broken glass lacerations, or corrosive chemical leachates) are detected, prominent high-contrast warning cards mandate protective gear and safe packaging.

5. Explicit Limitations

AI computer vision can misclassify heavily contaminated, crumpled, or multi-laminated packaging. The application displays an AI confidence meter and flags any predictions falling below 70% threshold.

6. Human-in-the-Loop

The platform explicitly directs users to contact certified municipal sanitation operators or authorized recycling facilities whenever safety-critical or non-standard industrial waste is involved.

Academic Innovation Methodology

The 5 Stages of Design Thinking

Structured human-centered approach followed throughout the 1M1B AI for Sustainability Virtual Internship.

Stage 01

Empathize

User Research & Observation

Conducted surveys among student dormitories and residential apartments to uncover the root cause of contaminated dry recycling bins.

Key Finding: 72% of participants wanted to recycle responsibly but were confused by ambiguous resin codes (like #5 PP vs #7 Other) and did not know whether soiled takeaway containers were recyclable.

Stage 02

Define

Core Problem Statement

Synthesized research into an actionable human-centric problem statement aligned with United Nations SDG 12.

Problem Statement: "Households and students need an instant, non-technical way to verify waste recyclability and handling safety at the bin side so that contaminated items do not ruin clean municipal recycling streams."

Stage 03

Ideate

Solution Brainstorming

Explored barcode scanning, hardware smart bins, chatbot assistants, and computer vision analyzers.

Chosen Concept: A web-first, zero-install AI assistant accepting both natural language queries and image uploads, featuring transparent explainability ("Why") so users learn long-term sustainable habits.

Stage 04

Prototype

Implementation

Developed the EcoSort AI single-file web application integrating simulated neural inference pipelines and curated waste knowledge engines.

Features Built: Multi-modal input (text/image), confidence indicator, low-confidence disclaimer banner, 10 benchmark test presets, and simulated community eco dashboard.

Stage 05

Test

Evaluation & Refinement
