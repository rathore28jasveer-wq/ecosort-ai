Waste Knowledge Base

Quick reference for common waste categories used in the prototype.

♻️ Recyclable

Paper, cardboard, clean metal cans and other materials accepted by a local recycling system.

🍌 Organic

Food scraps, fruit and vegetable peels and other biodegradable material suitable for local organic-waste systems.

💻 E-Waste

Electronic devices and accessories such as chargers, phones and certain batteries.

⚠️ Hazardous

Items that may require special handling, including certain chemicals, batteries or contaminated materials.

🧴 Plastic

Plastic packaging and containers whose disposal depends on material type and local collection rules.

🗑️ General Waste

Residual waste that cannot be placed in available recycling or organic streams.

Responsible AI Considerations

EcoSort AI is designed as decision support, with clear limitations and human verification.

⚖️ Fairness

Provide useful guidance across different users and waste descriptions.

🔍 Transparency

Clearly communicate that prototype classifications are not guaranteed to be correct.

🔐 Privacy

Uploaded images are previewed locally in the browser and are not sent to a server by this prototype.

🛡️ Safety

Hazardous waste should be handled through appropriate official or authorized channels.

👤 Human Verification

Users should verify important disposal instructions with local authorities.

⚠️ Limitations

Text rules and image previews do not replace a trained real-world AI vision model or local regulations.
